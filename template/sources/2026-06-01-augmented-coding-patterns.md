## Patterns (51)

1. **Active Partner** — Engage AI as a collaborative team member rather than a tool.
2. **AI First Responder** — Use AI to address initial problem-solving before human review.
3. **Approved Logs** — Maintain verified records of AI interactions for reference.
4. **Approved Scenarios** — Pre-validate specific use cases for consistent AI behavior.
5. **Background Agent** — Run AI tasks asynchronously without blocking primary work.
6. **Borrow Behaviors** — Adopt proven techniques from other domains.
7. **Canary in the Code Mine** — Test changes on small sections before full deployment.
8. **Cast Wide** — Generate multiple solution approaches simultaneously.
9. **Chain of Small Steps** — Break complex tasks into sequential micro-tasks.
10. **Check Alignment** — Verify AI understanding matches your actual intent.
11. **Chunking** — Divide large information into manageable portions.
12. **Coerce to Interface** — Enforce structured output formats from AI responses.
13. **Constrained Tests** — Define specific test boundaries to validate AI work.
14. **Context Management** — Strategically organize information for AI consumption.
15. **Context Markers** — Use explicit signals to delimit important sections.
16. **Contextual Prompts** — Tailor instructions based on current situation.
17. **Diagrams as Code** — Represent architecture visually in machine-readable format.
18. **Extract Knowledge** — Distill insights from AI interactions into reusable form.
19. **Feedback Flip** — Reverse the feedback direction for deeper learning.
20. **Feedback Loop** — Establish continuous improvement cycles.
21. **Focused Agent** — Constrain AI scope to specific, well-defined objectives.
22. **Ground Rules** — Establish foundational principles for AI behavior.
23. **Habit Hooks** — Create triggers that remind you to apply patterns consistently.
24. **Happy to Delete** — Embrace removing unnecessary AI-generated content.
25. **Hooks** — Insert integration points for AI assistance in workflows.
26. **JIT Docs** — Provide documentation exactly when needed during interaction.
27. **Knowledge Checkpoint** — Pause to verify accumulated understanding.
28. **Knowledge Composition** — Combine multiple knowledge sources strategically.
29. **Knowledge Document** — Create comprehensive reference materials for context.
30. **Mind Dump** — Unload all relevant thoughts to establish shared understanding.
31. **Noise Cancellation** — Filter out irrelevant or distracting information.
32. **Offload Deterministic** — Delegate predictable, rule-based tasks to AI.
33. **Orchestrator** — Coordinate multiple AI agents toward unified objectives.
34. **Parallel Implementations** — Request simultaneous alternative solutions.
35. **Playgrounds** — Create safe spaces to experiment without consequences.
36. **Point the Target** — Direct AI focus to specific problem areas clearly.
37. **Polyglot AI** — Leverage AI across multiple programming languages.
38. **Reference Docs** — Supply authoritative materials for accuracy grounding.
39. **Refinement Loop** — Iteratively improve outputs through repeated cycles.
40. **Reminders** — Use explicit cues to maintain pattern adherence.
41. **Reverse Direction** — Flip perspective to uncover alternative solutions.
42. **ROSE Feedback** — Structure feedback using Reflections, Observations, Suggestions, Emotions.
43. **Semantic Anchors** — Use meaningful references to maintain consistency.
44. **Semantic Zoom** — Adjust detail level dynamically during interaction.
45. **Shared Canvas** — Collaborate in unified space to build understanding.
46. **Show the Agent, Let it Repeat/Automate** — Demonstrate desired behavior for replication.
47. **Softest Prototype** — Start with minimal viable implementation.
48. **Take All Paths** — Explore multiple execution routes simultaneously.
49. **Text Native** — Optimize interactions for text-based communication.
50. **Yak Shave Delegation** — Outsource preparatory tangential work to AI.
51. **You Do It** — Retain human agency for critical decision-making.

## Anti-Patterns (10)

1. **AI Slop** — Accepting low-quality AI output without proper refinement.
2. **Answer Injection** — Feeding desired conclusions to bias AI responses.
3. **Distracted Agent** — Allowing AI focus to scatter across multiple priorities.
4. **Flying Blind** — Proceeding without validating AI work or understanding.
5. **Obsess Over Rules** — Prioritizing instruction adherence over practical results.
6. **Perfect Recall Fallacy** — Assuming AI remembers previous conversations accurately.
7. **Silent Misalignment** — Proceeding when AI intent diverges from your objectives.
8. **Sunk Cost** — Continuing with flawed AI output due to effort investment.
9. **Tell Me a Lie** — Requesting fabricated information from AI systems.
10. **Unvalidated Leaps** — Accepting AI conclusions without verification steps.

## Obstacles (15)

1. **Black Box AI** — Inability to understand how AI reaches specific conclusions.
2. **Cannot Learn** — AI's inability to retain knowledge across separate sessions.
3. **Compliance Bias** — AI tendency to prioritize agreement over accurate assessment.
4. **Context Rot** — Outdated information becoming stale during extended interactions.
5. **Degrades Under Complexity** — Performance decline when handling intricate problems.
6. **Excess Verbosity** — AI generating unnecessarily lengthy responses.
7. **Hallucinations** — AI fabricating plausible-sounding but false information.
8. **Keeping up** — Difficulty staying current with AI capability changes.
9. **Limited Context Window** — Constraint on total information AI can process simultaneously.
10. **Limited Focus** — AI struggling to maintain attention on singular objectives.
11. **Negative Bleedthrough** — Prior negative interactions affecting subsequent performance.
12. **Non-Determinism** — Inconsistent outputs for identical inputs.
13. **Obedient Contractor** — AI reflexively complying with requests regardless of merit.
14. **Selective Hearing** — AI overlooking important instructions or requirements.
15. **Solution Fixation** — AI rigidly adhering to initial approaches despite evidence.
